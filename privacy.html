PRIVACY POLICY

Plain-language and honest. This page matches the actual code — every storage key listed below is one the site really writes, verified against the source.

THE SHORT VERSION

No cookies. No analytics. No advertising trackers. No fingerprinting. Your settings and game progress live in your browser only, and the fonts are hosted by the site itself. The one optional third-party request (the live visit counter) runs only after you accept it in the banner. You can erase everything this site stores on your device with one click, and request deletion of your cloud account by email.

1. Who is responsible

Khalifa (operating as “HOLY”) — operator of this portfolio site.
Contact for anything in this policy, including deletion requests: HVoid9@proton.me (PGP fingerprint on the contact page).

2. What this site does NOT do

  • No cookies — the site sets none (no document.cookie usage exists in the code).
  • No analytics — no Google Analytics, no Matomo, no heatmaps, no page-view tracking. The optional visit counter (section 4) only increments an anonymous number and runs only with your consent.
  • No advertising or social-media trackers, no pixels, no fingerprinting scripts.
  • No third-party embeds — no YouTube, maps, or social widgets.

4. Data we hold in the cloud (Supabase)

Two things can leave your device, and both are things you actively do:

  • The visit counter — an anonymous, incremented number. No IP address, no identifier, no cookie is stored with it. It runs only if you press ACCEPT ALL in the banner; with essential-only the page loads no library and sends nothing.
  • An account (only if you sign up in the arena) — your email address, password (stored hashed by Supabase, never readable by us), and the callsign you choose. Used for: signing you in, showing your callsign on scoreboards, and enforcing bans for abuse. Your email is never used for marketing — there is no mailing list.
  • Scoreboard rows — callsign + score for quizzes and exams you play while signed in.

Provider: Supabase (database, EU/US hosting per their infrastructure). Public API key used by this site is the publishable key; row-level security in the database limits what anyone can write — details in the security policy.

8. Your rights

You can ask, at any time, for: a copy of your data, correction of anything wrong, deletion, restriction of processing, or portability — and you may object to processing and withdraw consent (COOKIE SETTINGS in the footer of any page). We answer within 30 days. If you believe we mishandled your data, you may complain to your national data-protection authority.

9. Retention

  • Browser storage — until you clear it or use the delete button.
  • Contact emails — kept only as long as the conversation requires.
  • Accounts / scores — until you request deletion.

10. Legal basis

Functional storage (section 3): strictly necessary for the site to work. Optional requests (the visit counter): consent. Accounts and replies: contract and your request. Everything else: nothing — there is no other processing.

11. Children

The site is a professional portfolio, not directed at children, and knowingly collects nothing from anyone under 16. If you believe a child has created an account, request deletion and it will be removed.
